FloodGame

FloodGame aims to create a web-based serious game geared towards educating K-12 and college students, and public on flood prevention and mitigation strategies, such that they are more informed about the implications of future flooding events.

How to Play

  • Press Start button to begin the game.
  • Select the tile and see its information.
  • If decided to apply a mitigation to selected tile, select the mitigation option from the Mitigation Options tab.
  • Game Progress and Goals sections give crucial information about the current situation.
  • Click Show Risks button to risky areas.
  • Hover mitigation or structure options to see details about them.
  • Watch the How to Play! for more information.

FloodGame

FloodGame is an educational game designed to teach K-12 students and college students about flood prevention and mitigation strategies to better prepare for future flooding events.

How to Play

  • Click the Next » button to select your game location.
  • Your goal is to minimize flood impacts by using mitigation strategies (relocation, parks, flood walls).
  • Monitor your Budget, Safety Score, and Population in the status bar up top.
  • Click the Risks button to view flood-prone areas.
  • Hover over mitigation or structure options to see descriptions.
  • Use the AI guide to learn and help with decision making!
  • Once the simulation starts, the game timer will run. Make your decisions strategically!
  • Watch the How to Play Guide for more details.
